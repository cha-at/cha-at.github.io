Josef Müller-Brockmann never had to fight Windows 98 for screen real estate,
but I like to think he would have respected the bevel.

## rules worth stealing

- the grid is the story; everything else is decoration
- one accent color, used like it costs money
- whitespace is not empty, it is load-bearing

> Order is not enough. You also need a little noise, or nobody stays.
> — me, just now, in this beveled window

## the compromise

Swiss gives the page its bones: the twelve columns, the type scale, the red.
Win98 gives it the skin: chrome, buttons, a status bar that lies about the
time less than most.

（慢慢来 — the grid can wait.)
