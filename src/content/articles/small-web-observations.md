Spent a week crawling webrings instead of feeds. Field notes:

- a 40 KB homepage can still make someone's afternoon
- guestbooks are just comment sections with manners
- every good tiny site has one page the author clearly loves more than the rest
- the "under construction" GIF was a promise, not a threat

The small web never died. It just stopped being the only web, and started
being the honest one.
